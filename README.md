# EU Critical Raw Material Dependencies

Quantitative research project investigating European Union dependence on critical raw material imports, supplier concentration, diversification and strategic resilience.

## Status

Research design phase.

## Objectives

- Measure supplier concentration
- Analyze diversification
- Identify changes over time
- Evaluate methodological limitations
- Explore geopolitical and strategic implications
